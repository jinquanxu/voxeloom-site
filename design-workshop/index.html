Three sessions, all in one week

Your pixel, your design, your set.

1
Your pixel · 50 minutes

Your photographs and the story behind them. We put them through our design studio while you watch, as voxels and then in LEGO® bricks, and decide together how big it should be, what must be kept, and whether you want the whole building, its front, or the rooms inside too.

2
Your design · 50 minutes

A day or two later, your design in real digital LEGO pieces. We turn it round and look inside together, and you choose: the colors, the roof, the door, the trees, the rooms and the people in them. Small changes are made while you watch; the rest before the last session.

Your finished LEGO® design and everything you need to build it. We show you how to order your bricks from LEGO with the Upload List, how to sort them by bag, and the first steps of the instructions, and answer your questions.

Then you build it

Order, sort, build

  1. You order the bricks from LEGO. Upload your list to LEGO’s Pick a Brick shop and it fills your basket with every piece. You check out and pay LEGO directly, for the bricks, their shipping and any tax.
  2. LEGO ships them to you, usually in one to two weeks. The pieces arrive loose, so the bag labels help you sort them.
  3. You build it, bag by bag, with your instructions.

Every piece in your design is one LEGO sells there, checked again on the day of your last session. If a piece sells out before you order, write to us and we will suggest a swap.

A full set from us, too? Order a full set of your design from us within 60 days of your last session — every LEGO® brick counted into numbered bags, with the instructions — and you receive $250 off its price. Instead of ordering the bricks yourself, or as a second set to give.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need experience or software?

No. You need a computer or tablet with Zoom, your photographs and an idea of what you would like. We do the designing on our side, with you watching and choosing.

Can I design any building?

Anything up to about 5,000 pieces: the front of a house, a whole cottage, a small church or a lighthouse. A larger landmark, or a fully furnished interior of a big house, needs more design time, and we will tell you the price before you book.

Are the bricks included?

No. The $499 covers the three sessions and your design’s files only. You order the LEGO® bricks yourself from LEGO’s Pick a Brick shop with your Upload List, and pay LEGO for them, their shipping and any tax, when you are ready. LEGO sets the prices, the stock and the delivery, and limits how many of one piece you may buy at once. We do not sell or ship bricks for the workshop, unless you order a full set of your design from us.

How do I book and pay?

Tell us about your place. No payment is taken on this page. We reply within two working days to confirm what fits and send the Zoom link; you pay by check, and once it clears we book your three sessions, all within one week.

Are the sessions recorded?

Not unless you ask us to. Your photographs are used only for your design. See our privacy page.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Tell us who it is for, and we will arrange the sessions with them.
