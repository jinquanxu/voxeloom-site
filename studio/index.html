From photographs to bricks

Pick a place and press Design it. You will watch it made the way every Voxeloom design is made: the photographs read as pixels, the place laid out in voxels, and every voxel turned into real LEGO pieces — an heirloom.

These are replays of designs we made from photographs, so nothing here is charged or ordered. Designing your own place right here opens soon. Until then, email us the photographs and one of us will design it.
