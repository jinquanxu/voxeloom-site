Carrie Tower (Small)

Brown University's Carrie Tower in real LEGO parts, the smaller of its two sizes: the red-brick campanile with five grooves up each face on its limestone base, with the inscriptions and the narrow door up three steps; four verdigris clocks stopped at twelve under a bracketed cornice; the balustrade and its urns, and the open belvedere of clustered columns under a small green dome. Around it a smaller corner of the green: lawn and a path, a shade tree and a tree in blossom, the black iron fence between brick piers along Waterman and Prospect Streets, a lamp post, benches and a tour group. Inside, the vestibule with the memorial plaque, an iron spiral stair up the shaft, the clock room with the movement and its rods to the four dials, and the bells.

Lighting

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

The tower is hollow from the vestibule to the belvedere, with its stair, so there is room inside to run the wiring to the clock faces, the bells and the lamp post.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
