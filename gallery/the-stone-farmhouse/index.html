The Stone Farmhouse

A two-storey stone farmhouse in real LEGO parts: warm tan masonry with white quoins, sills and a stone arch, under low terracotta tile roofs with a tall chimney and black gutters. Dark brown shutters stand open at the upstairs windows, a vine in white flower climbs beside the arched glass doors, and lanterns light the lower wing that steps forward on the right. On the left a vine-covered pergola shades the supper table; in front, a gravel courtyard, a lawn with stepping stones, an olive tree, cypresses and a bed of lavender behind a low stone wall with its lamps. Inside, the kitchen with its range and island, the living room with a lit fireplace and the long table laid for supper, a wine store with barrels and a potting room; upstairs, the main bedroom, a bathroom and the children's room.

Lighting

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

The farmhouse is hollow, room by room on both floors, so there is room inside to run the wiring to the windows, the lanterns and the garden lamps.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
