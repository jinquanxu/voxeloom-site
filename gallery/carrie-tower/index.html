Carrie Tower

Brown University's Carrie Tower in real LEGO parts, the centrepiece of its corner of the green: the red-brick campanile with five deep grooves up each face, on a buff stone base with its benches, festoons and narrow door; the four verdigris clocks under a bracketed cornice; the balustrade and its urns, and the open belvedere of paired columns under a green dome. Around it, lawn and paths, shade trees and a tree in blossom, the black iron fence between brick piers along Waterman and Prospect Streets, lamp posts, benches and a tour group. Inside, the vestibule with the memorial plaque, an iron spiral stair up the shaft, the clock room with the movement turning all four dials, and the old bells at the top.

Drag to take the model apart and see the rooms inside.

Lighting

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

The tower is hollow from the vestibule to the belvedere, with its stair, so there is room inside to run the wiring to the clock faces, the bells and the lamp posts.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
