The Windmill at Hammersmith Farm

Digital design with every piece checked against LEGO’s own Pick a Brick shop.

The Windmill at Hammersmith Farm in real LEGO parts: a guest house in Newport, Rhode Island, built to look like a windmill without its sails. An eight-sided tower of grey shingle with a flared skirt at the second floor, a white balustraded balcony round the windowed room at the top, and a dark bell-shaped cap with its chimney; beside it a square three-storey block with a roof terrace, and a low wing. In front, a dry-stone wall with a bed of yellow flowers, a path across the lawn and big leafy trees. Inside, a summer afternoon: a sitting room with a boy and his toy boat, a bedroom with a girl reading by the window, a study with a telescope trained on the bay, people with binoculars in the room at the top and out on the balcony, deck chairs on the roof terrace, and the cook in the kitchen.
