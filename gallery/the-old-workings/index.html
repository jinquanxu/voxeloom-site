The Old Workings

A mine nobody has worked in a long time: a propped adit into the hillside and an open cut beside it, ore still showing in both walls, the tramway out of the mouth going to rust and then to nothing, a cart on its side in the dirt, the headframe over the shaft with a brace missing, and weeds through the sleepers.
