Rhode Island State House

The Rhode Island State House in white marble: long three-storey wings either side of a central section with arched bronze doors and four columns, an attic with four colonnaded cupolas, the drum and its colonnade, the marble dome and lantern, and the gilded Independent Man on top. Inside, the rotunda with its stairs and balcony, the Senate and House chambers, the State Room and the State Library.
