Rhode Island State House

The Rhode Island State House in real LEGO parts: white marble wings of three storeys, the pavilion's arched bronze doors and columns, the ribbed dome on its colonnaded drum among four cupolas, the gilded Independent Man, the terrace and the grand steps. Inside, the rotunda under its painted dome, the House and Senate chambers with their members, the State Room, and the two-storey State Library.

Drag to take the model apart and see the rooms inside.

Lighting

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

The wings, the rotunda and the drum are hollow, with their floors and rooms, so there is room inside to run the wiring to the windows, the chandeliers and the lantern.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
