The Stone and Cedar House

A long, low contemporary house in fieldstone and vertical cedar, built as a house rather than a block: a storey-and-a-half wing with the front door retreated under a little dormer, a two-storey centre proud under a shallow cedar gable, a monitor of six windows lighting the middle of the house, a garage bent back at an angle with the cars inside, rooms on both floors, a pergola behind, and the lawn falling to the road over a stone retaining wall.
