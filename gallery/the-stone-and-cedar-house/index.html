The Stone and Cedar House

The Stone and Cedar House in real LEGO parts: fieldstone and vertical cedar, the wing's roof climbing to a monitor of six windows beside the stone chimney, the two-storey centre proud under a shallow gable, the front door up steps under its own little gable, the garage bent back at an angle, rooms inside, and the drive looping round a planted island.
