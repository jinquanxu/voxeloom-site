Rose Island Light

Digital design The pictures are renders of the design, with every piece checked against LEGO’s own shop.

Rose Island Light in real LEGO parts: the white clapboard keeper's house of 1870 under a grey slate mansard with two rust-red bands and arched white dormers, its brick chimney, and the square white tower rising from the roof to a black gallery and lantern, the lens inside under a domed cap. The house stands on the old fort's stone wall, railed in white, with the flag and deck chairs on top; below it the brick fog-signal house and its wooden steps, rock ledges, a strip of sand and the water of Narragansett Bay. Inside, as it was about 1912: the parlour with its player piano, the kitchen with the range and the cat, the pantry pump, bedrooms under the mansard, a ladder up the tower through the watch room to the lens, and the keeper's son at the engine in the fog-signal house.

Lighting

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

The house, the tower and the fog-signal house are hollow, with their floors and rooms, so there is room inside to run the wiring to the lantern, the windows and the deck.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
