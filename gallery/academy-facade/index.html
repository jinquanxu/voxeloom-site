The Academy (Façade)

Digital design with every piece checked against LEGO’s own Pick a Brick shop.

The Academy (Façade) in real LEGO parts: a school's front on a grassy rise, on a shallow base. At its center, a portico of four white columns under a white pediment, and above it a white cupola with an open belfry, a gold bell, a dark bell-shaped dome and a finial. Behind the columns, a red brick front with white window frames and two glass doors under a curved white hood. On either side, a cream pavilion with a triple window on each floor, then a lower wing, cream on one side and gray on the other. In front, a green bank with a flight of concrete steps up the middle, two black lamp posts, shrubs, three young trees and seven students. The back is a plain wall, so it sits neatly on a shelf or against a wall.
