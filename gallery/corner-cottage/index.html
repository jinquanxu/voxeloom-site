Corner Cottage

Digital design The pictures are renders of the design, with every piece checked against LEGO’s own shop.

A stone gate lodge in autumn, in real LEGO parts: a tower under a slate pyramid, a steep slate roof with a dormer and a green-gabled bay, the porch with its lantern, a glass conservatory, the tall chimney, a gravel drive, the gatepost and golden trees. Inside, the parlour by the fire, a kitchen with its range, a spiral stair up the tower, and bedrooms under the roof.

Drag to take the model apart and see the rooms inside.

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
