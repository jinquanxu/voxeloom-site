Warwick Neck Light

Warwick Neck Light in real LEGO parts: the white cast-iron tower on the bluff with its black gallery, its lantern and green light, the keeper's house of white clapboard under steep red roofs with its porch and brick chimney, the little garage, the flag and the Adirondack chairs on the lawn, and the stair down the bluff to the seawall and the keeper's skiff. Inside, a spiral stair up the tower to the keeper at the lens, the parlour and the kitchen, bedrooms under the roofs, and buoys being painted in the garage.

Lighting

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

The tower, the house and the garage are hollow, with their floors and rooms, so there is room inside to run the wiring to the lantern, the windows and the porch lamp.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
