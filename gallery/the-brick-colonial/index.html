The Brick Colonial

Digital design The pictures are renders of the design, with every piece checked against LEGO’s own shop.

A brick colonial in real LEGO parts: warm taupe brick with cream sills, lintels and cornices under a grey hip roof; the entry stands forward under a pediment, its black door under a fanlight in a cream arch between two lanterns, the arched window over it; a bay under its own little roof, white double-hung windows in pairs, and the wing with its dormer and a two-car garage facing the drive at the side. A lawn mown in stripes, beds of boxwood, white hydrangeas and arborvitae along the house, a shade tree at the corner and tall trees behind, a lamp post at the walk, a patio at the back and two air-conditioning condensers by the house. Inside, the two-storey foyer and stair, the dining room with the children at their homework, the living room, the kitchen and the family room by the fire, three bedrooms and two baths upstairs, a bonus room over the garage, and the attic under the roof: insulation between the joists, the air handler and its ducts, and a technician at work on it.

Lighting

A light kit is planned for this design when it is ordered.

The house is hollow, room by room on both floors and in the attic, so there is room inside to run the wiring to the windows, the lanterns and the lamp post.

A light kit is bought separately and never appears in the parts list. Press Lights on to see the model after dark.
