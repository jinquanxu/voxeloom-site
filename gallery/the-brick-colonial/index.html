The Brick Colonial

Digital design The pictures are renders of the design, with every piece checked against LEGO’s own shop.

A two-storey brick colonial under a hipped shingle roof, built as a house: a round-headed front door under a brick gable with an arched window lighting a two-storey foyer, a box bay under a dark metal roof, a storey-and-a-half garage wing with a hipped dormer, a sunroom with a fireplace across the back, and furnished rooms on both floors.

Drag to take the model apart and see the rooms inside.
