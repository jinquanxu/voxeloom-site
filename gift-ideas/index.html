For family

For parents and grandparents

The house that holds the stories.

The front steps where everyone gathered.
The garden they tended, season after season.
The kitchen window, lit for you coming home.

Give it back to them in bricks, for a milestone birthday, an anniversary, or the move to somewhere smaller.

And if they are settling into a new room, in assisted living or a care home, let a little of home go too: a familiar roofline on the windowsill, and a place to begin when you visit. Build it together, a few bags at a time, or let us build it for them.

Think of: the family home, their childhood house, a favourite holiday cottage, the house they are moving out of.
