Questions

Before you ask

How long does it take?

A design comes back in minutes in the studio, or within two working days if we draw it by hand. Once you approve a quote, a kit usually ships in two to four weeks — sooner than the months a hand-built commission takes, because the design is done by machine and checked by a person.

What do you need from me?

Photographs are best: the front straight on, both corners, and one from across the street. An aerial view from a maps site helps with the roof. If you only have an address, say so and we will work from what we can see.

Do you keep my photographs?

Only while your quote is open, so the person designing it can see them. When a quote is closed or declined, the pictures are deleted. Designs made in the studio drop their photographs as soon as the design exists.

Why Voxeloom?

A voxel is a pixel with depth — the grid of coloured cells every model begins as, before a single brick is chosen. A loom is what turns loose thread into something a family keeps. Your house makes that journey here: a grid on a screen on Monday, a box of numbered bags by the end of the month.

Are these real LEGO bricks?

Yes. Every part is ordered from LEGO's own Pick a Brick shop, so your model is built from genuine LEGO® bricks and can be repaired with them years from now. We are an independent workshop, not part of the LEGO Group.
