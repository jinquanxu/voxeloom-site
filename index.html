Why we are quicker

A first look in a minute. A kit in days, not seasons.

A custom brick model usually takes months, because a designer is drawing it by hand and you are in a queue behind everybody else. We took the drawing out of the queue.

The design is done the day you ask

The studio shows you the set while you watch, and one of our designers works through the real design with you the same day, using advanced AI design tools. There is no waiting list, because there is no backlog of drawing to get through.

The instructions write themselves

Every step and every numbered bag comes out of the model itself — nineteen thousand bricks sorted into build order in about a second. Nobody draws a booklet by hand here.

Every piece is a piece LEGO sells

The design is checked against LEGO's own shop while it is being made, so ordering the bricks is one upload rather than a hunt through substitutes. Nothing gets designed that cannot be bought.

Which leaves the post as the only real wait. Once you are happy with the design, the bricks are ordered from LEGO's Pick a Brick shop and their delivery is the long pole — usually a week or two. Large models take longer, because LEGO caps how many of one piece you may buy at a time and the order has to go in more than once.

What you get

What is in the box, and how paying works

No part of this should be a surprise. Here is exactly what arrives, and exactly when money changes hands.

Every model includes

  • Every brick. Genuine LEGO®, bought new from LEGO's own shop, sorted into numbered bags in build order.
  • Building instructions. Step by step, generated from your model, with the part list for each step — as a PDF by email, or printed for an extra fee.
  • The design itself. Pictures of your model from every side, and the full parts list, before you commit to anything.
  • As many changes as it takes. The roof, the colour, the tree in the wrong place — until it looks like the place you know.
  • A baseplate, so it stands where you put it.

Ask for it and we will

  • Build it for you, and send it assembled and ready for the shelf rather than as a kit.
  • Open it up. Rooms inside, furnished, with lift-off floors so you can see in. This is what the Heirloom tier is.
  • Light it. Lighting is bought as a kit and quoted separately — it never appears in the brick price. Say so early: a model that is going to be lit is designed differently from the start.
  • Put it in a case, with a plaque.

You pay once, when you have seen it

Nothing is owed while we design it, and nothing while you change your mind about the roof. When the model is right and you say yes, the price you approved is paid in full — and that is the moment the bricks are ordered.

There is no deposit and no booking fee, because you have already seen exactly what you are buying.

Prices are for the model. Shipping is quoted with your design, since it follows the weight and where it is going.

Questions

Before you ask

How long does it take?

The studio shows you your place in LEGO® bricks, in about a minute, and one of our designers works through the real design with you the same day. After that the wait is LEGO's rather than ours: the bricks come from their Pick a Brick shop and usually take a week or two, while the instructions are generated the moment the design is settled. A very large model takes longer, because LEGO caps how many of one piece you may buy at once and the order goes in more than once. A hand-drawn commission elsewhere takes months — the drawing is the part we removed. We take on only as many as we can do well, first come, first served, so if your gift must arrive by a date, count on that date once we have confirmed it to you in writing.

What do you need from me?

At least one photograph if you can, and up to ten: the front straight on, each side, the back, and one from above — the aerial view is the only way to get a roof right. If you have none to hand, the address is enough and we will look up the map views ourselves.

How do I reach you?

On the Talk to us page, send photographs and a few words, ask us to call you back, or leave your email and we will write to you. Or write to orders@voxeloom.com. Our Customer Care team reads every message and replies within two working days.

Do you keep my photographs?

Only while your quote is open, so your designer can see them. When a quote is closed or declined, the pictures are deleted. The studio uses your photograph only to render your pictures.

Why Voxeloom?

A voxel is a pixel with depth — the grid of coloured cells every model begins as, before a single brick is chosen. The loom is from heirloom: a thing made well enough to be handed down. A house makes the whole journey here: photographs on Monday, a grid on a screen that afternoon, a box of numbered bags by the end of the month.

Are these real LEGO bricks?

Yes. Every part is ordered from LEGO's own Pick a Brick shop, so your model is built from genuine LEGO® bricks and can be repaired with them years from now. We are an independent workshop, not part of the LEGO Group.
